Last updated: 26 November 2026

Welcome to RG GAMER BOY FF. We respect your privacy and aim to handle information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website, use its account features, interact with website content, or visit an external service through our links.

RG GAMER BOY FF is an independent gaming information and entertainment website. It provides gaming-related content, game descriptions, version and platform information, guides, featured content, and links to external game and download destinations.

You can browse the main gaming website without creating an account or making a payment. Account sign-in is an optional feature for visitors who choose to use it.

We do not place advertisements directly on our own website pages. However, external websites linked from our pages may display their own advertisements.

2. Information Processed When You Visit the Website

When you visit the website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by your browser and the website hosting provider. This may include:

  • IP address and general network information.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages or resources requested.
  • Date, time, and basic request information.

This information may be used to deliver website content, maintain availability, protect the service, and troubleshoot technical problems.

You do not need to provide your name, email address, or account details simply to browse the main gaming pages.

3. Information Processed When You Sign In

If you choose to use Google Sign-In, Google handles the authentication process. Depending on the permissions and information made available by Google, our sign-in service may receive information such as:

  • Your Google account display name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your profile picture URL, if available.
  • A unique account identifier used to recognize your account.

We do not receive or store your Google account password. Your Google password is handled by Google during its authentication process.

When you successfully sign in, our account database may store or update information associated with your account, including:

  • Your account identifier.
  • Your sign-in provider.
  • Your display name.
  • Your email address, if provided.
  • Your profile picture URL, if provided.
  • Your selected region and country.
  • The date and time of your latest sign-in.

This information may be stored even when some details, such as your email address or profile picture, are not displayed on your account screen.

4. How We Use Account Information

Information associated with your account may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the sign-in service.
  • Recognize your account when you return.
  • Connect your sign-in session with the correct account.
  • Display relevant account information.
  • Remember your selected region and country.
  • Maintain account functionality and service security.
  • Identify and troubleshoot account-related errors.
  • Respond to account support and deletion requests.

We do not sell visitors' personal information.

Information required for account functionality may be processed and stored by our account database service. The service may handle account identifiers, sign-in details, profile information, region and country selections, and sign-in activity information described in this policy.

Google is responsible for the Google account authentication process. Our account service is responsible for handling the account information that our website sends to it.
