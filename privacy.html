Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Welcome to RG GAMER BOY FF. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, use its features, sign in to an account, or visit an external website through one of our links.

1. About RG GAMER BOY FF

RG GAMER BOY FF is an independent gaming information and entertainment website. Our website provides game information, descriptions, version details, platform information, guides, and links to selected games and external download destinations.

Our website includes the following sections:

  • Home: Featured games and recently highlighted content.
  • Gaming: Online and featured games with platform and version information.
  • Tips & Guide: Helpful information and instructions related to listed games.
  • Download: Game listings, descriptions, and available external download links.
  • Website Details: Information about RG GAMER BOY FF and its features.

Browsing the main gaming website is free. You do not need to create an account or make a payment simply to browse its main pages. We do not display advertisements on our own website pages.

2. Information We May Collect

We aim to collect only information needed to operate the website and its optional account features.

When you browse the main website:

  • Your browser may send basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested page, and request time, to the website hosting provider.
  • Information may be processed to deliver website pages, maintain availability, and help identify technical problems.
  • You are not required to provide your name, email address, or other personal details just to browse the main gaming pages.

When you choose Google Sign-In:

If you voluntarily use the sign-in feature, we may receive basic account information made available through Google, such as:

  • Your display name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your profile picture, if available.
  • A unique account reference used to recognize your signed-in account.
  • Your selected region and country.
  • Basic sign-in activity information, such as the time of your last sign-in.

We do not receive your Google account password. Google handles the Google account authentication process under its own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

Information described in this policy may be used for the following purposes:

  • To display and operate the website correctly.
  • To provide optional Google account sign-in.
  • To recognize returning signed-in users.
  • To display basic account information where applicable.
  • To remember your selected region and country.
  • To maintain website security and help prevent misuse.
  • To respond to bug reports and website-related requests.
  • To troubleshoot errors and improve website reliability.

We do not sell visitors' personal information.

4. Region and Connection Information

The sign-in page allows visitors to choose a region and country. This selection is used to provide the relevant account and connection display features.

The website may make periodic connection requests to display a response-time measurement. This measurement is intended to show the connection response time of the service and is not a complete internet speed test.

Any displayed internet speed estimate depends on information made available by the visitor's browser and may not be available on every device.

Your selected country is a selection made by you. It is not proof of your actual physical location.

5. Browser Storage and Sign-In Sessions

Our website and sign-in page may use browser storage for basic functionality.

  • To remember acceptance of the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • To remember your selected region and country.
  • To maintain a sign-in session when you choose to use Google Sign-In.
  • To support the temporary transition from the sign-in page to the main website.

You can clear browser data through your browser settings. Doing so may reset saved preferences or require you to sign in again.

6. Human Verification

Some external destinations may require human verification before the website redirects you to the selected destination.

Our website uses hCaptcha for this verification feature. When the verification service is loaded, its provider may process technical information such as browser details, device information, IP address, and interaction information to help determine whether a request is genuine.

hCaptcha operates under its own privacy policy and terms:

  • hCaptcha Privacy Policy
  • hCaptcha Terms

When you open an external link, you leave our website and visit a service operated by another provider.

Those external websites may:

We do not control the privacy practices, content, availability, or security of external websites. Please review the relevant website's policies before using it or submitting personal information.
