Privacy Policy

RG GAMER BOY FF

Last Updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

RG GAMER BOY FF respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website or use our account sign-in service.

2. Your Sign-in Server

When you use our account sign-in service, our Sign-in Server may maintain a basic account record containing:

  • Your unique account ID
  • Your sign-in method
  • Your display name
  • Your selected region and country
  • Your account sign-in date and time

This information helps our service recognize your account and maintain your sign-in session.

3. Google Account Information

When you choose to continue with Google, Google processes the information needed to authenticate your Google Account and provide the sign-in service.

Depending on the information made available by Google, this may include:

  • Your Google Account identifier
  • Your display name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photo URL

Our current sign-in system may also send the available display name, email address, and profile photo URL to our account database as part of your account record.

We do not receive or store your Google Account password.

4. Information We Do Not Ask For

We do not ask users to provide their Google password to our website. We do not request payment card details for Google sign-in.

5. Browser Storage and Sign-in Sessions

Our website may use browser storage to remember your selected region, country, privacy-policy acceptance, and sign-in session.

This helps provide a consistent experience when you return to the website.

6. Technical Information

When you visit our website, the hosting and network services involved in delivering the website may process basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and connection timestamps.

Our sign-in page may also make connection checks to measure service responsiveness.

7. External Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, game pages, download sources, or community platforms. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

8. Account Deletion Requests

If you want to request account deletion, use the account deletion request option provided on our sign-in page. Submitting a request does not itself confirm that deletion has been completed. The request must be reviewed and processed.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended to provide gaming-related information. Users should not submit unnecessary personal information. Parents or guardians can contact us if they have concerns about information submitted by a child.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or account services change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or account requests, please use the contact method provided on the RG GAMER BOY FF website.

Thank you for using RG GAMER BOY FF.
