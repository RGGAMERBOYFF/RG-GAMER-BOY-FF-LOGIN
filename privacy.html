Last Updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RG GAMER BOY FF. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website or use our account sign-in service.

2. Information Stored on Your Sign-in Server

When you use our account service, our Sign-in Server maintains account information needed to recognize your account and manage your sign-in session.

This account record may include:

  • Your unique account ID
  • Your sign-in method
  • Your display name
  • Your selected region and country
  • Your account sign-in date and time

3. Google Account Information

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google processes information required to verify your Google Account and complete the sign-in process.

Depending on the information made available by Google, this may include:

  • Your Google Account identifier
  • Your display name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photo URL

Our current sign-in system may also send the available display name, email address, and profile photo URL to our account database as part of your account record. These details are not necessarily displayed on the website's account screen.

We do not receive or store your Google Account password.

4. How We Use Account Information

Account information is used to recognize returning users, maintain sign-in sessions, display basic account details, and operate the account features of our website.

5. Browser Storage and Sessions

Our website may use browser storage to remember your privacy-policy acceptance, selected region and country, and sign-in session. This helps provide a consistent experience when you return.

6. Technical Information

Our website hosting and network services may process basic technical information when pages are requested. This may include IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and connection timestamps.

Our sign-in page may also perform connection checks to measure service responsiveness.

7. External Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, game pages, download sources, and community platforms. These external services operate under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

8. Account Deletion Requests

If you want to request account deletion, use the account deletion request option provided on our sign-in page. Submitting a request does not automatically confirm that your account has been deleted. The request must be reviewed and processed.

9. Children's Privacy

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information. Parents or guardians who have concerns about information submitted by a child may contact us through the contact method provided on our website.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or account services change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or account requests, please use the contact method provided on the official RG GAMER BOY FF website.
